Circle Vibe | Hackathon

2024
See Details

Circle Vibe is an app designed to encourage civic engagement and help communities identify and address problems in their environment. Through it, users can report issues affecting their community, such as damaged streets, fallen trees, or other needs, adding a description, location, and photos to facilitate identification. It also allows users to view problems reported by others, learn about implemented solutions, review the results, and find local services that can help resolve these situations. Its main objective is to connect citizens, promote collaboration, and facilitate the search for solutions to improve their communities.

Agrored | Expo

2025
See Details

AgroRed is a web platform designed to connect wholesale buyers and sellers of agricultural products in El Salvador, facilitating product marketing and improving buying and selling processes. The project aims to solve common problems in the agricultural sector, such as lack of inventory control, logistical difficulties, and a lack of information on product quality and availability. Its features include a product catalog with updated photos, purchase requests and agreements between sellers and buyers, as well as logistics plans to facilitate deliveries.

Finans | ExpoTech

2026
See Details

Finans is a digital financial management platform designed to facilitate the administration of micro and small enterprises (MSEs), helping them to better control their financial resources. It allows users to record income, expenses, savings, and debts, consult statistics, and generate reports. In addition, it integrates an artificial intelligence assistant called Kuali, which provides guidance on business finances and offers predictions based on recorded data. The project aims to simplify financial management through accessible, intuitive technological tools tailored to the needs of small business owners.
